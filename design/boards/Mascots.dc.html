MASCOT IDEAS
Six ways to give the planning assistant a face
Bobbin
THE ARCH
The arch from the logo, wearing a thread scarf.
Good: Matches the logo exactly and is the easiest to draw in every pose.
Watch out: The plainest of the six.
Needle
THE NEEDLE
A tall needle with the gold thread through its eye.
Good: Says sewing and precision at a glance, and the eye is a natural place for the thread.
Watch out: The pointed shape is less cuddly.
Spool
THE SPOOL
A spool of gold thread with a loose end.
Good: A sturdy shape. The loose thread can unwind as a progress bar for the plan.
Watch out: Could read as a sewing shop.
Skein
THE BALL OF THREAD
A round ball of gold thread with a trailing end.
Good: The softest and friendliest, closest in feel to a classic app mascot.
Watch out: The thread lines get busy at small sizes.
Wren
THE SMALL BIRD
A small bird carrying a gold thread.
Good: Warm, and it can fly in with reminders. A bird mascot is familiar to people.
Watch out: Closest to the Duolingo idea, so keep it distinct with the thread and colours.
Button
THE BUTTON
A button with a stitched smile.
Good: Tiny and clear. Works as a favicon and chat avatar.
Watch out: The least character, so fewer poses.
