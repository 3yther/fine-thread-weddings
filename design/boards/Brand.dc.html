The logo ink is now pure black to match the page. Keep clear space equal to the width of the arch. Smallest size for the mark is 40 px. Never recolour the thread, stretch the arch, or add religious or couple symbols. On the homepage the name is also used as the cropped hero wordmark.
IMAGERY
Painted images only, as circular crops or one full-bleed panel. Photos are never shown as photos. Subjects: flowers, gardens, architecture, interiors, landscapes and textiles. Avoid scenes of one tradition's ceremony, so the site feels open to every couple. Source photos are free Pexels images, turned into paintings with a brushstroke renderer. The Pexels licence allows free commercial use, but check each photo and avoid showing identifiable people. Replace them with real weddings once couples and photographers agree.
