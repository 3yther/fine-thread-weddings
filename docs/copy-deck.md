## Tradition notes (internal, not for the site as legal claims)
- Islamic: a nikah alone is not always a legally recognised marriage in England and Wales. A civil registration is also needed. Point couples to GOV.UK.
- Christian: Church of England weddings need banns or a licence and a qualifying connection to the parish. Point couples to the church and GOV.UK.
- Never give legal advice. Say what to check and link to the official source.
